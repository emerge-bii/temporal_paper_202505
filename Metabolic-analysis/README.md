# Metabolic analysis scripts for the EMERGE temporal paper

## 00_MAG_overview

Overview of MAGs, including ordinations, heatmaps and correlations between genomes.

## 02_DRAM_distillate

Analysis of DRAM-based annotations of MAGs, including pathway summaries, stability over time, alternative respiration, heatmaps and summary of top MAGs for each habitat-depth.

## 03_redox_cycles

Bullseye diagrams of carbon, nitrogen and sulfur cycling pathways, including abundance-based and expression-based analyses at mire-, habitat- and habitat-depth-wide scales.

## 04_copresence_network

Correlations and network analysis based on MAG abundance.

## 05_metaT_analysis

Analysis of metaT expression data, including at reaction, pathway, metapathway and genome levels.
Includes a deep dive on chitin degradation.

## 06_specialisations

Categorisation of MAGs into specialities, including methanogens, methanotrophs, acetogens, carbon generalists, etc.

## 07_amino_acid_frequency

Amino acid frequency analysis, comparing EMERGE MAGs to GTDB.

## 08_RDA

dbRDA analysis of MAG abundance compared to habitat, depth, time and temperature.
